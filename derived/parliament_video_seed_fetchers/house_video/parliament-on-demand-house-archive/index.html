How to access information and updates

  • To watch the House (both live and on-demand), please visit Live Video | New Zealand Parliament. If this link does not work, you can watch on Freeview 31 and Sky 86.
  • To view the Order Paper, please go to Parliament's social media channels.
  • To view Oral Questions, please visit Oral Questions - New Zealand Parliament . If this link does not work, it will be available on Parliament's social media channels.
  • To view the schedule of meetings for Select Committees, please go here: Schedule of Meetings - New Zealand Parliament. If this link does not work, it will be available on Parliament's social media channels.
  • To create or sign a petition, please go here: Petitions - New Zealand Parliament
  • For information on how to make a submission, please check our social media channels.
